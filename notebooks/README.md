# Notebook map

The repository has one reader-facing final study and a numbered sequence of reproducible experiments.

- [Final study](final/uk_bands_punching_above_weight.ipynb): the executed 100-band analysis of current global Spotify reach and scene depth across the ten largest UK Functional Urban Areas
- [Experiment catalogue](experiments/README.md): experiments 01–23, with the question each notebook tests and why it matters

Preserved superseded work remains under [`archive/`](archive/). The September 2025 publication is in [`archive/published-v1/`](archive/published-v1/), the original scratchpad is in [`archive/original-analysis/`](archive/original-analysis/), and the abandoned Google Trends route is in [`archive/google-trends/`](archive/google-trends/).
